WHY THIS MATTERS

The expedition robot leaves for Everest Sept 5. Above 8,000 m the only connection is ~2 kbps of Iridium — video needs 1,000× more. NeverDrop streams a live digital twin through that soda straw, survives blackouts with onboard store & forward, backfills every missing sample, and when the robot crashes its built-in black box bursts the last 10 s home as a 3D reconstruction with a root cause. Every byte here crossed a real rate-limited link as framed 340-byte packets.
